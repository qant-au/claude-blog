# Adam Burgess — Author Bio

Adam Burgess is a Brisbane-based strategic technology consultant with thirty
years of senior ICT experience across Australia, the Asia-Pacific, and the
Middle East. He started as a Queensland Police intelligence officer, studied
theoretical physics at the University of Canterbury, and built one of the
broadest technical careers in the Australian ICT industry — covering
programming, infrastructure, network architecture, cybersecurity, project
management, and senior consulting.

His delivery record includes a twelve-million-dollar network overhaul across
six major Queensland stadiums, airport infrastructure deployments, government
data centre migrations, and international client engagements across APAC and
the Middle East. He is currently co-founder of VMAAS Australia (cybersecurity)
and runs his consulting practice through QANT Pty Ltd, while building a small
portfolio of men's health and income brands.

Adam writes about men's health, decision making, critical thinking,
technology, and what it takes to stay financially relevant after fifty. He
does so not as someone who has arrived, but as someone who is still building —
in public, with honesty, and without the performative tone that usually
attaches itself to this kind of content. The position is simple: nobody is
coming to fix this for you, and the sooner you accept that, the sooner the
useful work starts.

He lives in Brisbane with his wife and dogs. He has a physics degree he has
never used in a lab and uses every single day. He wants a sixty-foot
catamaran. He is working on it.
